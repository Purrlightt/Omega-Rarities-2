Astral Flux Mechanics

Astral Flux stats do not increase immediately. They start scaling at specific thresholds:

Bulk
Starts scaling at 100,000
Speed
Starts scaling at 10,000,000 (10M)
Luck
Starts scaling at 10,000,000,000 (10B)

1M Absolute Caps

Specific info regarding the 1M Absolute rune's maximum potential:

  • This Rune (1M Event) Stays Forever, because the Update (Upd 1) Was Balanced using this rune.
  • At around 108k, it maxes out **everything except Luck**.
  • At around 76.25k, it maxes out **Rune Luck** and **Rune Bulk**.

Event Rune Boost Rules

Event Runes are unique and ONLY receive boosts from:

Gamepasses Potions The Rune itself

Other external multipliers do not apply to event rune stats.

Rare Rune Milestone
A tutorial for the rare rune milestone, merging RPS limits, luck settings, and bulk optimization.
Created by Purrlight

⚠️ Important: RPS Limit

Your actual RPS amount must be equal to or below the calculated number for your Rune Clones. If your RPS is higher than the requirement, it will not work!

1. Find Your Rune Clones

To use the calculator, you need your exact Rune Clone count. You can find this in your Profile menu inside the game.

2. Turn Off Luck

For the Rune Grind calculations to be accurate, you MUST have your Luck turned OFF. This ensures the base drop rates are used.

3. Tweak Your Bulk

Before starting your grind, ensure you tweak your Bulk amount in the settings to optimize your efficiency.
